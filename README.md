## About Me

I am **Shah Fazle Elahi**, a Full Stack Software Developer,
Technology Entrepreneur, AI Model Trainer, SaaS Product Developer,
and Legal Researcher based in Sylhet, Bangladesh.

I am the Founder of **Assetify Hub Technologies**.

My technical interests include scalable software architecture,
enterprise applications, SaaS platforms, artificial intelligence,
business automation, cloud computing, and digital transformation.

With an interdisciplinary background in law, technology,
research, and entrepreneurship, I am particularly interested in
developing practical solutions to complex real-world problems.

I believe in continuous learning, responsible innovation,
research-driven development, and the meaningful application
of technology.
